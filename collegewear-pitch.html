What we're seeing

Performance

Pages taking 15–25 seconds

🚫
Deployment

Every design session that didn't convert left behind a WooCommerce product record and media files. Your database scans through years of abandoned sessions on every page load.

The firm that built it in 2019 has zero original staff left. You don't have access to your own codebase. Changes happen reactively, in the dark.

Why AI can't help you yet

Your data isn't ready
to answer questions

When someone asks "why did orders drop last week?" — nobody can answer that in under an hour. Not because the data doesn't exist. Because it lives in five different places and doesn't talk.

1

Orders split across two WooCommerce instances

Stoles and partner are separate platforms. No single view of the business. Cross-site analytics means a manual export.

2

Odoo knows things WooCommerce doesn't — and vice versa

Production status lives in Odoo. Order intent and design details live in WooCommerce. A fragile, race-condition-prone webhook connects them. When it breaks, nobody knows.

3

Ghost data corrupts every query

An AI querying your database right now would treat abandoned design sessions from 2021 and test orders as real data. Garbage in, garbage out.

4

No observability layer

You find out about outages when a customer emails. No metrics pipeline, no alerts, no historical performance data. You can't improve what you can't see.

5

Custom data lives in a WordPress black box

Stole design options, school configs, color choices — serialized key-value blobs inside wp_postmeta. No structure, no indexes, can't be queried meaningfully.

How we think about this

Stabilize first.
Then make it intelligent.

We don't parachute in with an AI solution before the foundation exists. That's how you end up with a fancy dashboard on top of broken plumbing.

01
Understand

We spend real time listening first

Before we touch a line of code, we want to know how your team works, what decisions you make every day, and where you're flying blind. The best AI answers the questions you're already asking — just instantly.

02
Audit

Map everything — platform, data, integrations

Not to write a report nobody reads. To know exactly what's broken, what's good enough, and what needs a rebuild. Your team already has fixes written — we'd start by getting those shipped safely.

03
Unify

One place where all your data lives clean

WooCommerce orders (both sites), Odoo production status, design configurations, school data — brought together, cleaned, structured properly. This is what makes everything else possible.

04
Intelligence

An agent that understands your business

Not a chatbot. A system that knows your data model, knows which engine to query for which question, and returns plain English answers with the reasoning shown. You ask — it figures out where to look, runs the query, and tells you what it found.
